How I work

Four habits I try to keep, from lab to demos.

  • Clean data first Strong baselines before fancy models. Evidence over vibes.
  • Measure carefully Ablations, error analysis and clear evaluation before I claim a win.
  • Keep people in mind Design sense plus AI depth. Systems that feel usable, not just accurate.
  • Stay close to the field Hackathons, London Tech Week and builder communities keep the work grounded.
